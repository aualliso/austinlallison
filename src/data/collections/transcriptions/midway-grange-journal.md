Midway Grange No. 1351
Midway, Hunt Co., Texas
Oct. 6th, 1883

    The first meeting of this Grange af-
    ter organization was held on Saturday
    in the fourth degree. Bro. Jonathan
    Guice of Ray's Creek acting Worthy
    Master Pro. Tem. Bro. Jno Woods of
    the same order acting. W.S. On ex-
    amination by the A.S. and L.A.S
    all were found correct, and every thing
    then pronounced in ample order.
    Mrs. Callie Shipp was then iniatated
    as a Charter member in the four degree.
    Motion to meet on the 1st Saturday
    in each month at 2 o'clock P.M., also
    on the 3rd Saturday in each month at 
    7 o'clock P.M. adopted. The bus-
    iness of the day being concluded the
    meeting adjourned to meet again Sat-
    urday Oct. 20th.

Midway Grange No. 1351. Nov. 3rd, 1883

Grange opened today with
a small attendance. Several officers not
present and the following members elect-
ed in their stead. Geo. Lane, W.O. J.W.
Lane, Steward. Mrs. N.C. Swann, Chaplain,
Mrs. S.F. Lane, Flora. No lecturer nor 
treas. elected as there were not enough
members present to fill all the offices.
The minutes of last meeting were read
and adopted. Mr. Hade Girdner ap-
plied for membership by [demit] and
was unanimously admitted into the Grange.
It was suggested for the good of the
order that all be punctual in attendance.
Reading of minutes next in order.
The business of the day being declared
over, the Grange was formally closed
to meet Saturday, Nov. 17th, 1883.

Midway Grange, No. 1351. Nov. 17th, 1883
Midway, Hunt Co., Tex.

Grange opended today according to
previous appointment. All present ex-
amined and found correct. Our W.S. 
being absent. G.F. Lane was duly appointed
in his stead for the day. Mrs. S.F. Lane
apponted Ceres, and Mrs. Callie Shipp
appointed Pamona. A committee of Messrs.
W.F. Lane, A.J. Wommack, and D.E. 
Shipp. The minutes oflast meeting read.
Messrs S.L. Shipp, W.O. Dunbar and
G.F. Lane appointed as a standing 
committee. Standing committee in-
structed to obtain material for fur-
nishing house with curtains and desk.
Committee composed of D.E. Shipp, A.J.
Wommack and N.A. Swann appointed to
construct bylaws for this Grange. The
meeting thne adjourned in due order to
meet again Saturday, Dec. 1st, 1883.

Done by N.A. Swann
Secretary

Signed W.M.
